# Marketing notes

Features worth putting in front of composers, as the owner flags them. Each
says what it does in a composer's words, then why it matters. Keep it honest:
only what ships.

## Headline: Quick Playback

**Hold to hear. Let go to keep writing.**

Every staff has a quick playback key. Hold it down and you hear that staff
alone, from where you are looking, on its own instrument; keep holding and the
score plays on. Let go and you are back exactly where you were writing - the
cursor and playhead never move.

Why it matters: picking the next note means hearing the last few. No other
notation program lets you check one line in context without losing your place
or touching the mix's solo buttons.

## Also worth telling

- **Notation first, everywhere.** Ostinatos, chord progressions and templates
  are written on the staff as real notation (spelled in the key, tied at the
  barlines) and play from there - not dropped in as MIDI to be cleaned up.
- **Chords in one click.** Click a note on the staff and the chord on that note
  appears, in your key, spelled correctly: triad, seventh, maj7, add9, sus2,
  sus4, diminished, augmented; root position or any inversion; close or open,
  over one octave or two. On piano it is written for two hands.
- **Hear notes as you place them** (one key to turn it off).
- **Templates by scene**: horror, comedy, action and 200 more - every track laid
  out, one per instrument or one per articulation.
- **A floating track list**: find an instrument by name and jump straight to it.
- **Work view and Score view**: the edit window as big as it gets, or the full
  score as it prints, one key away.
- **Your studio**: any Core Audio interface (Universal Audio, MOTU, RME,
  Focusrite...), USB-C MIDI keyboards found as they are plugged in, Mackie
  Control mixer controllers (SSL UF8, Avid S1, Icon, X-Touch, FaderPort),
  outboard gear in an insert with automatic latency, the mixer on its own
  touch screen.
- **Always up to date**: new builds install from inside the app; plug-ins are
  checked at start-up.
